# The 2ksbox name and logo

2ksbox's code is free software under the GPL-2.0: you may use, study,
share and change it, and distribute your builds, as the licence says.
The licence covers the code, not the name. "2ksbox" and its logo are
trademarks of Roboport Tecnologia (David Rios Gomes Ltda), used to tell
people which builds come from this project.

**You may, without asking:**

- say your work is based on, compatible with or a build of 2ksbox
  ("an unofficial build of 2ksbox", "based on 2ksbox");
- use the name in articles, videos, reviews and tutorials;
- package 2ksbox for a distribution or a package manager unchanged, or
  with the changes the distribution needs, under the name 2ksbox,
  provided the package's description says it is packaged by the
  distribution and not by this project.

**Not allowed:**

- publishing a build with other changes, or an app in any store, under
  the name 2ksbox or a confusingly similar one; pick your own name, and
  say it is based on 2ksbox;
- using the 2ksbox logo for a modified build;
- suggesting that this project made, checked or endorses your build.

Questions: open an issue.
