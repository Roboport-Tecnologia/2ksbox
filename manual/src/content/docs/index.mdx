<CardGrid>
	<Card title="A machine library" icon="laptop">
		One window makes a Windows 98, XP, 7, 11, DOS or other machine
		with settings that suit it. Each machine opens in its own window.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Period 3D, accelerated" icon="rocket">
		DirectX 1 to 9 and OpenGL run on your graphics card, and an
		emulated 3dfx Voodoo 2 runs Glide games with 3dfx's own driver.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Your discs" icon="document">
		cue/bin, CloneCD, Alcohol, ISO, or a folder. Copy-protected discs
		and CD audio work. Swap discs while the machine runs.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A CRT look" icon="seti:image">
		The guest's picture at its own resolution, through a CRT shader
		you can tune, instead of a blurry stretched rectangle.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
